## Three findings

- The dataset covers Sep 2016 to Oct 2018 and records 96,478 delivered orders, with revenue of 13,221,498. The period includes partial months: Sep 2016, Oct 2016, Dec 2016, Sep 2018, Oct 2018.
- Late orders received lower reviews: average reviews were 2.27 for late orders and 4.29 for on-time orders; the reported review gap is 2.02 (CI: 1.98–2.06). **Lateness and reviews are correlated, not proven cause and effect.** The reported late rates differ: 6.8% and 8.1% by timestamp.
- Repeat purchasing is limited: the repeat rate is 3.0%, and month1 retention is 0.5%. Freight accounts for 16.6% of revenue; the top three categories account for 25.9%.

## Three recommendations

- Prioritize delivery improvements in AL, MA, SE, PI, and CE. **Expected impact:** the what-if scenario estimates 286 late orders and 152 low reviews avoided. **Assumption:** the scenario’s interventions work as modeled in those 5 states.
- Test customer-retention initiatives. **Expected impact:** 934 extra repeat customers and 127,939 revenue per repeat point. **Assumption:** the supplied per-point estimates apply if the repeat rate increases.
- Review freight costs in the high-freight categories, starting with christmas_supplies and signaling_and_security. **Expected impact:** the supplied freight-reduction estimate is 32,979. **Assumption:** the proposed review can realize that estimate without harming customer experience or sales.

## Risks

The period contains partial months, limiting month-to-month comparisons. Delivered orders without a delivery date (8) are excluded from lateness metrics, and 610 products lack a category. The what-if and per-point impacts are estimates, not guaranteed outcomes; monitor results before scaling.
